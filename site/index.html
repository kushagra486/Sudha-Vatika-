About us

A venue for your family's biggest days

Sudha Vatika is a family-run venue on the old Kanpur Road in Dhoomanganj, Prayagraj, celebrating weddings, parties, events, functions and meetings. Everything is in one place: a grand pandaal, an air-conditioned hall, an open lawn, rooms and a dormitory for your guests (our guest house), and a decor team to dress it all for your function.
