About us

One venue for every part of your celebration

Sudha Vatika is a family-run wedding and event venue on the old Kanpur Road (N.H.-02) in Dhoomanganj, Prayagraj. Weddings, receptions, engagements, birthdays, family functions and corporate meetings are all hosted here, from an intimate gathering to a grand wedding of 1,500 guests.

Everything is in one place: a grand pandaal, the air-conditioned Ashoka Hall, an open lawn, a meeting and conference hall, VIP rooms and a dormitory for the baraat and family. Our decor team dresses the entrance, the passages and the stage to your theme.

Talk to Atin Gupta to plan your function: visit us, call, or send a WhatsApp message any time.

The Guest House

A comfortable stay, right at the venue

Our guest house keeps the family and the baraat together under one roof, a few steps from the function. Guests can rest, freshen up and get ready without travelling across the city between ceremonies.

  • VIP rooms: 3 rooms ready now, with more opening soon.
  • Comfortable rooms: double beds, extra beds for families, attached bathrooms with hot water.
  • Dormitory: shared stay for larger groups and relatives.
  • Easy and quiet: a calm sitting area, away from the noise of the function.

Meals are not included with the stay. Food and catering are arranged only on your request, so you choose what suits your function.
